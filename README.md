**Musalli** is your essential Islamic companion, designed to help you stay connected with your daily prayers and achieve complete focus and mindfulness (_Khushu_). Combining precise prayer time calculations with an innovative **automated Rak'ah counter**, Musalli ensures you never miss a prayer or lose count during your Salah.

Whether you need accurate Adhan notifications wherever you are in the world, or a reliable assistant to eliminate doubt and distraction regarding the number of prostrations (_Sujud_) and units (_Raka'at_), Musalli offers an intuitive, lightweight, and clutter-free experience.

### 🌟 **Key Features:**

*   **Smart Rak'ah & Sujud Counter:** An innovative, hands-free tracking system that accurately counts your Rak'ahs and prostrations as you pray. Say goodbye to distraction, doubt, and forgetfulness during Salah.
    
*   **Precise Prayer Times Worldwide:** Accurate calculation of all five daily prayers based on your exact location, supporting all major Islamic calculation methods (Umm al-Qura, Muslim World League, Egyptian General Authority, ISNA, and more) with manual adjustment support.
    
*   **Customizable Adhan Notifications:** Timely and melodious Azan alerts with voices from renowned Muadhins, ensuring you are always reminded when prayer time enters, even in background or silent modes.
    
*   **Accurate Qibla Compass:** A built-in digital compass showing the precise direction of the Holy Kaaba from anywhere on the globe without delay.
    
*   **Daily Athkar & Digital Tasbih:** Complete collection of morning and evening remembrance, post-prayer Athkar, and Hisn al-Muslim, alongside a seamless digital counter for daily Dhikr.
    
*   **Battery-Efficient & Modern Design:** A clean, modern interface featuring smooth navigation and eye-friendly Dark Mode. Built with lightweight architecture to ensure zero unnecessary battery drain.
    

### 💡 **Why Choose Musalli?**

*   **Offline Functionality:** Core features, including prayer times and the Rak'ah counter, work reliably without requiring constant internet access.
    
*   **Privacy Focused:** Respects your privacy without requesting intrusive or unnecessary permissions.
    
*   **Continuously Updated:** Regular performance enhancements and feature refinements based on user feedback.
